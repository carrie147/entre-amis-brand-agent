## Name

**Entre Amis** — French for "between friends". `CONFIRMED`

- Always written as two words, both capitalised: **Entre Amis**.
- Never: "EntreAmis", "Entre amis", "entre-amis" (the last is allowed only in
  URLs, handles and file names).
- Do not translate the name in brand output. It may be explained ("French for
  'between friends'") where helpful.
