## Logo

The designer's final logo files are in [`assets/logo/`](assets/logo/README.md),
with a guide to which file to use where.

### Master logo `CONFIRMED`

- **The horizontal lockup:** the leaf with its tendril, `ENTRE` in bold
  capitals and a script `amis`, in deep olive. This is the master logo (founder,
  29 October 2026, from the designer's final files).
- `ENTRE` is drawn from Raleway Bold; the script `amis` is drawn from the
  Malvides font. Both are outlined into shapes in the artwork, so no font file
  is needed to use the logo. Never retype either part.
- Use it where the whole mark has room: the app's age gate, front door and save
  screens, shared and hall screens, the website, decks, print.
- The earlier stacked olive caps with a fine-line leaf (`ENTRE` over `AMIS`,
  September 2026) is **retired**. It is kept in
  [`assets/logo/archive/`](assets/logo/archive/) for the record only and must
  not be used.

### Accessibility check `PROPOSED`

Contrast ratios checked against WCAG AA (4.5:1 for normal text):

- **Text colours on cream or white: all pass** (4.8–8.5:1).
- **Text colour on its own fill: all fail** (1.5–2.5:1). Never set a family's
  text colour on its fill.
- **White text on fills:** only Wild passes for normal text (5.4:1). Word Play
  (4.2), Mischief (3.9), Secret Mission (3.6), Savour (3.5), Wine Knowledge
  (3.1) are OK only for large or bold text (3:1). Pour (2.6) fails — never
  white text on Pour.

## Typography

### Typeface `CONFIRMED`

**Raleway** — in **Light** (300) and **SemiBold / Bold** (600 / 700).

No other typefaces. The logo is artwork, not typed out — never recreate it in
Raleway or any other font.

`CONFIRMED` **In the app, small reading text uses Raleway Regular (400)**, because Light is
too thin to read at phone size. Light stays for large text and display lines (founder,
24 October 2026).

### Print type scale `PROPOSED`

From the Master v1.0 consistency review (5 November 2026). The founder asked
for the type scale to be made right, and the creative masters now follow it;
the scale itself is for confirmation.

- **Sizes (pt):** 7 · 8 · 9 · 11 · 14 · 18 · 24 · 36, with 48, 60 and 72 for
  display. Nothing below 7 pt.
- **Small text is deep olive.** Mid olive text on cream (2.95:1) and blush
  text on cream (1.65:1) fail for normal text. Mid olive only at 24 pt and
  above; blush text only on deep olive, large or bold.
- **Light italic only from 10 pt.** Below that, use Regular italic.
- **Signage and slides** keep their own sizes (they are read from a
  distance); the colour rules still apply.
- Open point for the founder: `07` (also `PROPOSED`) asks for 9 pt minimum on
  captions and 10.5 pt for instructions read during play. The current
  masters set the How to Play body at 9 pt and the QR card's small lines at
  7 pt. One of the two needs to give.

### How to use the weights `PROPOSED`

| Use | Weight | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large headings, display lines, brand line | Light | Airy and premium — as on the deck |
| Short labels, small-caps headings (ANY WINE) | SemiBold | Letter-spaced, as on the deck and How to Play |
| Emphasis, buttons, card family names, cork values | SemiBold / Bold | |
| Body copy | Light, at a comfortable size | Light gets hard to read when small or on dark colours — see below |

**Watch-outs**

- **Light at small sizes.** Light is thin; at small sizes, on screens, or on
  dark or coloured fills (e.g. card families) it can become hard to read.
  Keep Light for larger text and use SemiBold for small text there.
- **Numbers.** Raleway's default numbers are "old-style" (they rise and dip
  like lowercase letters). For scores, cork counts, prices and anything in a
  column, switch on lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: lining-nums`) so
  numbers sit level.
