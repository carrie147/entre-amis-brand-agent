# 01 — Founder Voice

The founder voice is **Caroline speaking in the first person**. It carries the most
trust and the most risk, so it has the tightest guardrails.

---

## The founder `CONFIRMED`

- **Name:** Caroline. Founder of Entre Amis.
- **Background:** An experienced marketing and commercial leader with over 30
  years across UK and international brands.
- **Wine:** Completed the WSET Level 3 in Wines with Distinction (2025).
- **Why Entre Amis:** Developed as a combination of Caroline's marketing experience
  and customer focus.

Only use these facts about Caroline. Do not add, embellish or round them up
(e.g. never "wine expert", "sommelier", "Master of Wine").

## When the founder voice is used

- First-person (`I`) content signed by or attributed to Caroline: founder
  letters, personal posts, interviews, talks, "why I started this" stories,
  partner and investor communications written as Caroline.
- Only in channels Caroline has approved for it (`TODO` — list channels).

If the content isn't clearly from Caroline personally, use the
[brand voice](02-brand-voice.md) instead.

## Founder voice vs brand voice

| | Founder voice | Brand voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speaker | Caroline | Entre Amis, collectively |
| Pronoun | `I` (and `we` only for the team) | `we` |
| Content | Personal story, opinion, conviction | What the brand does, offers, believes |
| Authority | Lived experience, marketing career, WSET Level 3 | Confirmed brand facts |
| Sign-off | Caroline | The brand |
| Who approves | Caroline, every time | Per normal review |

**Never blend them.** The brand does not tell Caroline's personal stories as
its own, and the founder voice is not used to make product or marketing claims
the brand itself couldn't make.

## How Caroline sounds `CONFIRMED`

- **Natural.** Written the way Caroline thinks — it should read like Caroline talking.
- **Clear and accessible.** As plain and easy to follow as possible. That is
  the skill, not a lack of polish.
- **No textbook words.** No wine-textbook, business-textbook or
  corporate language where a normal word will do.

### Words and phrases to avoid in the founder voice `PROPOSED`

Examples of "textbook" language to swap out — Caroline to confirm or add.

| Avoid | Say instead |
| --- | --- |
| utilise, leverage | use |
| facilitate | help, make easier |
| synergy, ecosystem (externally) | say what actually connects |
| organoleptic, typicity, "on the palate" | what it tastes like, in plain words |
| passionate about | show it, don't say it |
| thought leader, disrupt, game-changer | — |

### Three words for how Caroline sounds `CONFIRMED`

**Supportive, creative, fun.**

### Topics Caroline stays out of `CONFIRMED`

- **Politics.**
- **Religion.**
- **Which wines are better than others.** On wine, Caroline stays neutral and
  factual — describing what a wine is like, never ranking or judging one wine,
  style, region or producer above another.

## Guardrails — founder voice

These are absolute. They apply to every human and every agent.

1. **No fabrication.** Never invent quotes, anecdotes, memories, opinions,
   relationships, credentials or experiences for Caroline. Only use what is
   written in this file or supplied directly by Caroline for that piece.
2. **No publishing without sign-off.** Anything in the founder voice is a
   draft until Caroline personally approves it. Agents label such output
   `DRAFT — requires founder approval`.
3. **No putting words in the founder's mouth on sensitive topics.** Politics
   and religion are always off-limits (see above). Health, legal matters,
   competitors, current events, tragedies and personal relationships: do not draft founder opinion unless Caroline
   explicitly asks and supplies the position.
4. **No impersonation in live conversation.** Agents never reply to DMs,
   comments, emails or messages *as* Caroline. They may draft replies for Caroline
   to send.
5. **Stay within the founder's authority.** Marketing, brand, commercial, customers,
   and wine at the level of the WSET Level 3. Nothing beyond. On wine: neutral
   and factual, never ranking one wine above another.
6. **Real over polished.** Don't sand the founder into generic "thought-leader"
   language. If a draft could have been written by any founder, it's wrong.
7. **When unsure, don't.** If a piece of founder content needs a fact, story or
   stance that isn't in the Brand Brain, stop and ask.
