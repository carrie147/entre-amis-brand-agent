# 01 — Founder Voice

The founder voice is **one real person speaking as themselves**. It carries the
most trust and the most risk, so it has the tightest guardrails.

---

## When the founder voice is used

- First-person (`I`) content signed by or attributed to the founder: founder
  letters, personal posts, interviews, talks, "why I started this" stories.
- Only in channels the founder has approved for it (`TODO` — list channels).

If the content isn't clearly from the founder personally, use the
[brand voice](02-brand-voice.md) instead.

## Founder voice vs brand voice

| | Founder voice | Brand voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speaker | One named person | Entre Amis, collectively |
| Pronoun | `I` (and `we` only for the team) | `we` |
| Content | Personal story, opinion, conviction | What the brand does, offers, believes |
| Authority | Lived experience | Confirmed brand facts |
| Sign-off | Founder's name | The brand |
| Who approves | The founder, every time | Per normal review |

**Never blend them.** The brand does not tell the founder's personal stories as
its own, and the founder voice is not used to make product or marketing claims
the brand itself couldn't make.

## Guardrails — founder voice

These are absolute. They apply to every human and every agent.

1. **No fabrication.** Never invent quotes, anecdotes, memories, opinions,
   relationships, credentials or experiences for the founder. Only use what is
   written in this file or supplied directly by the founder for that piece.
2. **No publishing without sign-off.** Anything in the founder voice is a
   draft until the founder personally approves it. Agents label such output
   `DRAFT — requires founder approval`.
3. **No putting words in their mouth on sensitive topics.** Politics,
   religion, health, legal matters, competitors, current events, tragedies and
   personal relationships: do not draft founder opinion unless the founder
   explicitly asks and supplies the position.
4. **No impersonation in live conversation.** Agents never reply to DMs,
   comments, emails or messages *as* the founder. They may draft replies for
   the founder to send.
5. **Stay within their authority.** Only speak on the topics listed above as
   within the founder's expertise.
6. **Real over polished.** Don't sand the founder into generic
   "thought-leader" language. If a draft could have been written by any
   founder, it's wrong.
7. **When unsure, don't.** If a piece of founder content needs a fact, story or
   stance that isn't in the Brand Brain, stop and ask.
