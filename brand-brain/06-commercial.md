## Commercial hypotheses — not for external use `PROPOSED`

Recorded as hypotheses only. Not offered, not in development, and never
described to partners — not even as "coming". Same rule as
[early thinking](00-brand-core.md#early-thinking--not-for-external-use-confirmed).

### 1. A standard Entre Amis insight package

- Every paying Partner Mode and Event Mode partner receives a standard
  insight package as part of the commercial relationship.
- It covers engagement, wine preferences, discovery and participation, from
  that partner's own tasting or event, within the data rules in
  [03](03-guardrails.md#partner-mode-data-confirmed).
- **It stands on its own.** A partner with an in-house marketing team can
  interpret it and act on it without buying anything else.
- It is the "commercial definition of reporting" the Work Tracker lists as
  needed before selling Partner Mode.
- It can't be offered until reports for businesses are built and the founder
  makes them claimable.

### 2. An optional Clarity for Growth advisory

- For a limited number of partners, an optional monthly advisory from
  Clarity for Growth: about 2–3 hours of Caroline's personal time a month
  (founder's working estimate), interpreting permitted data, identifying
  commercial opportunities and recommending practical actions.
- It is access to senior commercial marketing judgement, not an enhanced
  dashboard or a more detailed report.
- Pricing, packaging and demand are unvalidated. No development is
  authorised.

### How the two differ

| | Standard Entre Amis insight | Optional Clarity advisory |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whose product | Entre Amis, inside the partner relationship | Clarity for Growth, a separate business |
| What it is | A report on the partner's own tasting or event, with plain notes on reading it and acting on it | Senior commercial judgement, delivered personally by Caroline |
| Who delivers | Entre Amis, the same for every partner of a type, without founder time | Caroline |
| Data | Anonymised and aggregated, from that partner's own tasting or event | See [separation](#entre-amis-and-clarity-are-separate-businesses-proposed) |
| Contract | Inside the Entre Amis partner agreement | Its own agreement, with Clarity |
| Who gets it | Every paying partner | A limited number, by choice |

## Entre Amis and Clarity are separate businesses `PROPOSED`

Entre Amis and Clarity for Growth are separate commercial businesses. Any
arrangement between them follows these rules:

1. Nothing in the standard insight package is held back to make the advisory
   worth buying.
2. A partner never needs the advisory to use or understand the package.
3. Clarity receives no personal data, and nothing a partner couldn't see
   itself, unless the founder confirms a further rule after consent and legal
   review (see [03](03-guardrails.md#third-businesses-and-partner-data-proposed)).
4. Competing partners' information stays confidential to each of them.
5. Entre Amis materials never present the advisory as a premium tier of
   Entre Amis.
6. What a partner pays Clarity for is judgement, never Entre Amis data.
7. Entre Amis never judges wines; any view Clarity gives a partner on its
   wines is Clarity's own.
8. Nothing moves between Entre Amis and Caroline's CMO brain without the
   founder's approval, and any approved borrowing is recorded here.
