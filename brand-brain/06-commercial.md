### Licensing rule `PROPOSED`

> Any licensing deal is about finding the best commercial route for the
> physical game, not surrendering control of the Entre Amis brand or assets.

Source: founder, 23 October 2026. It applies to every licence discussion and
every term below: the brand, the app, the platform, the data and the wider
Entre Amis ecosystem stay with Entre Amis, and any exception needs the
founder's explicit agreement.

### Hypotheses

- **Licensing over self-manufacture.** Licensing the physical game to an
  established manufacturer or distributor may be preferable to Entre Amis
  managing manufacturing, inventory, distribution and retail relationships
  itself. A strategic hypothesis, not a decision.
- **Opening royalty position: 20–25% of the selling price excluding VAT.**
  At the £40 RRP, if calculated against the ex-VAT retail price (£40 at 20%
  VAT is £33.33 ex VAT), that is about £6.67–£8.33 a game. An opening
  hypothesis from the discussion, not an established industry benchmark.
- **Scope of any licence:** the Entre Amis app, platform, data and wider
  brand ecosystem stay outside any physical-game licence unless explicitly
  agreed. (The commercial pack's licence terms say the same: physical game
  only.)

> **All figures are working hypotheses to resolve** (founder, 23 October
> 2026). That includes the commercial pack's 15% royalty and £8.50 unit
> cost, the 20–25% opening position and the May 2027 availability. None is a
> position to use with partners until the founder resolves it. The only
> confirmed figure is the £40 RRP in 00.

### 1. A standard Entre Amis insight package

- Every paying Partner Mode and Event Mode partner receives a standard
  insight package as part of the commercial relationship.
- It covers engagement, wine preferences, discovery and participation, from
  that partner's own tasting or event, within the data rules in
  [03](03-guardrails.md#partner-mode-data-confirmed).
- **It stands on its own.** A partner with an in-house marketing team can
  interpret it and act on it without buying anything else.
- It is the "commercial definition of reporting" the Work Tracker lists as
  needed before selling Partner Mode.
- It can't be offered until reports for businesses are built and the founder
  makes them claimable.

### 2. An optional advisory, offered by Entre Amis

- **Entre Amis holds the offer.** The founder's direction (23 October 2026)
  is a model in which Entre Amis includes the advisory in its own partner
  offer, to protect it and drive value, rather than Clarity for Growth
  selling it separately.
- For a limited number of partners, an optional monthly advisory: about
  2–3 hours of Caroline's personal time a month (founder's working
  estimate), interpreting the partner's insight, identifying commercial
  opportunities and recommending practical actions.
- It is access to senior commercial marketing judgement, not an enhanced
  dashboard or a more detailed report.
- **Data it uses:** the same insight the partner receives, combined with
  public or paid-for wine industry research and observations. Never anything
  that belongs to another individual Entre Amis partner.
- Pricing, packaging and demand are unvalidated. No development is
  authorised.

### How the two differ

| | Standard insight package | Optional advisory |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whose offer | Entre Amis | Entre Amis |
| What it is | A report on the partner's own tasting or event, with plain notes on reading it and acting on it | Senior commercial judgement, delivered personally by Caroline |
| Who delivers | Entre Amis, the same for every partner of a type, without founder time | Caroline |
| Data | Anonymised and aggregated, from that partner's own tasting or event | The same insight, plus public or paid-for wine industry research and observations |
| Who gets it | Every paying partner | A limited number, by choice |

## Entre Amis and Clarity for Growth `PROPOSED`

Entre Amis and Clarity for Growth are separate commercial businesses. With
the advisory held by Entre Amis, these rules apply:

1. Nothing in the standard insight package is held back to make the
   advisory worth buying.
2. A partner never needs the advisory to use or understand the package.
3. The advisory uses only the insight that partner receives, plus public or
   paid-for industry research and observations. No personal data, and
   nothing that belongs to another individual partner.
4. Paid-for research is used only as its licence allows.
5. Competing partners' information stays confidential to each of them.
6. The advisory is presented as a separate, optional service, never as a
   better version of the standard package.
7. What a partner pays for is judgement, never Entre Amis data.
8. Entre Amis never judges wines; the advisory never ranks one partner's
   wines against another's.
9. Clarity for Growth's wider fractional CMO and advisory business stays
   outside Entre Amis.
10. Nothing moves between Entre Amis and Caroline's CMO brain without the
    founder's approval, and any approved borrowing is recorded here.
