### Licensing rule `CONFIRMED`

Source: founder, 23 October 2026 (confirmed the same day). It applies to every licence discussion and

### Hypotheses

- **Licensing over self-manufacture.** Licensing the physical game to an
  established manufacturer or distributor may be preferable to Entre Amis
  managing manufacturing, inventory, distribution and retail relationships
  itself. A strategic hypothesis, not a decision.
- **Opening royalty position: 20–25% of the selling price excluding VAT.**
  At the £40 RRP, if calculated against the ex-VAT retail price (£40 at 20%
  VAT is £33.33 ex VAT), that is about £6.67–£8.33 a game. An opening
  hypothesis from the discussion, not an established industry benchmark.
- **Scope of any licence:** the Entre Amis app, platform, data and wider
  brand ecosystem stay outside any physical-game licence unless explicitly
  agreed. (The commercial pack's licence terms say the same: physical game
  only.)

> **All figures are working hypotheses to resolve** (founder, 23 October
> 2026). That includes the commercial pack's 15% royalty and £8.50 unit
> cost, the 20–25% opening position and the May 2027 availability. None is a
> position to use with partners until the founder resolves it. The only
> confirmed figure is the £40 RRP in 00.

## Commercial advisory panel and referral model `PROPOSED`

Founder's update, 23 October 2026. A proposed part of the Entre Amis

### Commercial advisory panel (hypothesis)

- A small, selective panel to support Entre Amis as it develops and
  commercialises, without a large permanent team or unnecessary fixed costs.
- Complementary expertise: buying, premium brands, retail distribution,
  licensing, wine industry relationships and commercial partnerships.
- Purpose: experienced challenge, opening relevant doors, and support on
  important commercial decisions.
- **Sarah**, an experienced Buying Director / Brand Director, is a potential
  founding member. A proposal, not an agreed appointment.

### Referral and introduction commissions (hypothesis)

Sarah, and potentially other approved commercial advisers, could earn
commission for introductions that result in successfully onboarded, paying
Entre Amis partners or customers. The model needs to settle:

| Element | To settle |
| --- | --- |
| Qualifying revenue streams | For example vineyard partnerships, events, commercial licensing, other B2B relationships |
| Qualifying introduction and conversion | What counts as an introduction, and as a successful, paying, onboarded partner or customer |
| Period | First-year revenue only, or a defined later period too |
| Rate | Relative to contribution, gross margin and cost of delivery (cost to serve, Work Tracker PO4) |
| Recording and approval | How introductions are recorded, attributed and approved. Written terms agreed before any commission is payable |

### Three separate roles

Each is agreed on its own terms. None automatically carries the same
remuneration as another.

| Role | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Advisory participation | Sitting on the panel: challenge, perspective, support on decisions |
| Paid project support | Specific, scoped pieces of work |
| Referral commission | Payment only for qualifying introductions that convert |

### Financial implications (illustrative, no figures)

Commission is a cost of winning revenue, not revenue. No rate is assumed
here; scenarios are shown as structures to fill once the founder sets
working figures.

| Scenario | What changes | What to assess |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partner or Event deal introduced by an adviser | Commission comes out of that partner's revenue | Partner profitability after commission and cost to serve |
| Licensing deal introduced by an adviser | Commission comes out of licence income (royalty and any guarantee) | Net licensing income; whether commission applies to the guarantee, royalties, or both, and for how long |
| Direct B2B customer introduced | Commission sits alongside other ways of winning customers | Customer acquisition cost, compared with winning the same customer without an introduction |
| No introduction | No commission | The baseline for each comparison above |

### Collaborations and Partner Mode

Founder, 24 October 2026:

- **Partner Mode is only for the tastings a partner hosts.** Its content
  reaches the guests at that tasting, not everyone on the app.
- **A Curated Collaboration reaches all app users**, not only one tasting.
  It is less about play than about content relating to that partner (for

The funding model and the consumer access model need not be the same.

### Editorial independence and brand protection `PROPOSED`

The founder's principles, for her to confirm. **Entre Amis retains control
over which producers, regions and organisations are featured: invitation or
selective approval, never open paid inclusion.** Funding supports worthwhile
experiences; it never buys preferential treatment.

1. Entre Amis retains editorial and creative control over the game
   experience.
2. Partners are selected for their relevance, quality, stories and fit with
   Entre Amis's brand values.
3. Collaborations are transparent about commercial involvement.
4. The content stays inclusive, playful and enjoyable, never advertising or
   a promotional brochure.
5. A paid collaboration does not imply that the partner's wines are
   objectively superior.
6. Entre Amis remains the lead experience brand, with the partner
   appropriately recognised.
7. Content and rights are agreed before publication.

The collaboration should feel like something Entre Amis has chosen to
create, not something a producer has bought.

### Assessment of the founder's questions (for review)

1. **Does the split strengthen the architecture?** Yes. It separates what
   Entre Amis makes for players (Mini-Editions, no partner, low risk) from
   what it makes with partners (Collaborations, governed by the principles
   above). That mirrors the existing split between the game and the app for
   players and Partner Mode and Event Mode for businesses, and keeps
   sponsorship out of the everyday game. The two overlaps (checks 6 and 7)
   are now settled by the founder.
2. **Umbrella name?** Recommendation: keep **Curated Collaborations** for
   partnered editions only, so "collaboration" always means a commercial
   partner is involved (principle 3, transparency). The family as a whole
   needs its own umbrella name, the founder's to choose; the name of the
   whole range isn't settled anywhere in the Brand Brain yet.
3. **Most promising first pilot?** Recommendation: a **free Seasonal
   Mini-Edition** first. It needs no payments, no partner, no rights
   agreement and no consent changes, and it tests the format and repeat play
   on its own. The first commercial pilot would then be a **Time-Limited
   Featured Edition inside an Event Mode relationship already in
   discussion**: the partner pays, access comes through the event, and no
   consumer payment is needed. Paid packs come last, because payments, a
   secure paid-access record and the pricing checks aren't built yet. No pilot
   runs with real players until the readiness level 1 gate (including the
   company being formed) is met.
4. **Invitation-led and premium, but welcoming?** The invitation applies to
   partners, never to players. For players: free or low-price access; plain
   English, no expertise needed; playable with any wine and a single glass;
   no edition required to enjoy the core game; partner content as discovery
   and stories, never as a sales pitch.
5. **Genuine dependencies on the existing app?** Known: optional packs are
   already a decision (FD5), and the app already runs an optional extra card
   level. Not known from here: whether the app can add, unlock, time-limit or
   restrict a pack to one event or partner. That is the technical review
   task. Genuine dependencies for paid packs: payments, a secure
   paid-access record and the codes check (G2), all still to build. The app
   is a web app today; a native app is on hold, so app-store rules matter
   only if that changes.
6. **Financial model without overstating income?** There is no financial
   model yet. When there is: Mini-Editions as engagement first, with direct
   revenue at zero in the base case and pack sales only as an upside
   scenario; free editions as a cost. Partner-funded editions as B2B
   scenarios only once a partner has agreed terms, never as forecast income.
   Show creation, review and maintenance costs against each scenario, and
   keep editions outside near-term income until a pilot has run.

## Commercial hypotheses — not for external use `PROPOSED`

### 1. A standard Entre Amis insight package

- Every paying Partner Mode and Event Mode partner receives a standard
  insight package as part of the commercial relationship.
- It covers engagement, wine preferences, discovery and participation, from
  that partner's own tasting or event, within the data rules in
  [03](03-guardrails.md#partner-mode-data-confirmed).
- **It stands on its own.** A partner with an in-house marketing team can
  interpret it and act on it without buying anything else.
- It is the "commercial definition of reporting" the Work Tracker lists as
  needed before selling Partner Mode.
- It can't be offered until reports for businesses are built and the founder
  makes them claimable.

### 2. An optional advisory, offered by Entre Amis

- **Entre Amis holds the offer.** The founder's direction (23 October 2026)
  is a model in which Entre Amis includes the advisory in its own partner
  offer, to protect it and drive value, rather than Clarity for Growth
  selling it separately.
- For a limited number of partners, an optional monthly advisory: about
  2–3 hours of Caroline's personal time a month (founder's working
  estimate), interpreting the partner's insight, identifying commercial
  opportunities and recommending practical actions.
- It is access to senior commercial marketing judgement, not an enhanced
  dashboard or a more detailed report.
- **Data it uses:** the same insight the partner receives, combined with
  public or paid-for wine industry research and observations. Never anything
  that belongs to another individual Entre Amis partner.
- Pricing, packaging and demand are unvalidated. No development is

### 3. Anonymised trend reports for the wine trade

## Entre Amis and Clarity for Growth `PROPOSED`

Entre Amis and Clarity for Growth are separate commercial businesses. With
the advisory held by Entre Amis, these rules apply:

1. Nothing in the standard insight package is held back to make the
   advisory worth buying.
2. A partner never needs the advisory to use or understand the package.
3. The advisory uses only the insight that partner receives, plus public or
   paid-for industry research and observations. No personal data, and
   nothing that belongs to another individual partner.
4. Paid-for research is used only as its licence allows.
5. Competing partners' information stays confidential to each of them.
6. The advisory is presented as a separate, optional service, never as a
   better version of the standard package.
7. What a partner pays for is judgement, never Entre Amis data.
8. Entre Amis never judges wines; the advisory never ranks one partner's
   wines against another's.
9. Clarity for Growth's wider fractional CMO and advisory business stays
   outside Entre Amis.
10. Nothing moves between Entre Amis and Caroline's CMO brain without the
    founder's approval, and any approved borrowing is recorded here.
