## How to use an insight `CONFIRMED`

- **Cite it every time:** source, date and, where known, sample size.
- **Quote it as the source reported it.** Don't combine, round or restate
  figures (e.g. "27% significantly more and 39.8% slightly more", not
  "66.8% more").
- **Say what it is.** A forecast is a forecast; a stated intention is not a
  purchase; a brand-commissioned poll is labelled as one.
- **Recheck before external use.** Before an insight goes into a partner
  pack, recheck it against the original and keep the date in view.
- **Never draw a line from the market to Entre Amis.** "People find wine
  lists intimidating" may be stated; "so Entre Amis will grow sales" may not.
  Sales, loyalty and partner return are pilot hypotheses.

## What the evidence does and doesn't show `CONFIRMED`

- **It supports** easier, less intimidating choice; choosing by taste and
  occasion; shared, meaningful occasions; drinking less but better; and
  interest in English wine and wine tourism.
- **It doesn't show** that Entre Amis will increase sales, loyalty, customer
  lifetime value or partner return. Those are pilot hypotheses.
- **North American Come Over October activations** are not UK or European
  evidence.
