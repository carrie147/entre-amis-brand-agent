# 02 — Brand Voice

How Entre Amis sounds when it speaks as a brand: website, product, packaging,
social, email, customer service, partner materials, and anything the brand
agent produces.

---

## Brand line and supporting lines

### Brand line `CONFIRMED`

> **Talk. Laugh. Remember.**

This is the line for Entre Amis as a whole brand.

### Supporting lines, in order of priority `CONFIRMED`

1. **The setting changes. The spirit never does.**
2. **Conversation, not knowledge.**
3. **Any wine. Any table. Everyone has something worth saying.**
4. **Say what you really think.**

Use them in this order of preference. Don't invent new brand-level lines —
propose them to the founder.

### Game and app slogans `CONFIRMED`

Slogans for the game and app are **descriptive, not set in stone, and may
vary** (e.g. "Pour. Spin. Play. Talk."). They describe the product, not the
brand, and must never be presented as the brand line.

## Tone principles

### Core tone — premium, accessible and never judgemental `CONFIRMED`

| We are… | …never | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Warm** | Worthy | Knowledge is shared generously, never used as status. |
| **Curious** | Corrective | We help people discover more; we don't tell them what they should have known. |
| **Premium** | Precious | Beautifully considered, but relaxed enough to actually use. |
| **Playful** | Silly | There is wit, competition and mischief, but it still feels grown-up. |

### Supporting principles `CONFIRMED`

These sit alongside the core tone. The core tone wins if they ever conflict.

| We are… | …but never | In practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct | Cold or curt | Say the thing plainly. Friends don't hedge or pad. |
| Generous | Salesy | People get more than they expect. Lead with what's useful or enjoyable for them, never with what we're selling. |
| Confident | Arrogant | State what's true without superlatives or put-downs. |
| Inclusive | Cliquey | "Between friends" invites people in; never an in-crowd. |

## Mechanics

- **British English, always.** `CONFIRMED` (judgemental, colour, favourite,
  organise, flavour, programme, £.)
- **French words carry their correct accents.** `CONFIRMED` (rosé, Provençal,
  château, crémant, côtes.) Never drop or approximate an accent.
- **Pronouns:** we / you. Never refer to Entre Amis in the third person inside
  its own copy ("Entre Amis believes…" → "We believe…"). `CONFIRMED`
- **Casing:** sentence case for headings, buttons and subject lines. Short
  small-caps labels (e.g. ANY WINE, EVERYONE PLAYS) are fine in design. `CONFIRMED`
- **Sentences:** short. One idea each. Read it aloud — if a friend wouldn't
  say it, rewrite it. `CONFIRMED`
- **Exclamation marks:** sparse and by exception — at most one per piece, and
  only when it genuinely belongs. Never forced in. `CONFIRMED`
- **Emoji:** allowed in social and connection pieces. Not used elsewhere by
  default. Always positive, never offensive — and never anything that hints
  at drunkenness or drinking more. `CONFIRMED`
- **Emoji set — the only emoji we use, everywhere:** 👍 thumbs up ·
  🤞 fingers crossed · 👎 thumbs down · ❤️ hearts · 🍷 wine glass · 😊 smiley ·
  😂 laughing · 🤔 thinking. `CONFIRMED`
- **French:** short, everyday French words where they fit naturally (e.g.
  *bientôt*), and wine terms. Always with the correct accents. Never as
  decoration. `CONFIRMED`

## Internal language — never external `CONFIRMED`

These are internal business terms. They must never appear in consumer,
partner-facing or public copy:

- GameCo, ExperienceCo, "the Group" or any group/company structure name
- "routes to value", "unit economics", "commercial models", "shareholder"
- "insight" as a business product (e.g. "community and insight")
- Platform or supplier names behind features (e.g. FastCork)

Brand architecture is still evolving (a Group may become an umbrella brand).
**When it changes, this list must be updated before anything new is used.**

## Voice by context `CONFIRMED`

Same personality, different volume. Across every context:

- **Never selling.** Nothing should feel like a sales push.
- **Never intrusive.** We don't chase, nag or interrupt.
- **Easy, never hard.** The game is designed to be easy; everything we say
  about it should feel easy too.
- **Playful, never overly silly** — especially in marketing communications.
- **Don't lock into a time of day unless there's a reason to.** People may
  play at lunch or in the evening. Default to time-neutral words (table,
  company, today, the occasion). Only name a time of day when the context
  needs it — or the app knows it.

| Context | Dial | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social | Warmest, most playful | Still no invented claims |
| Website / product | Clear first, warm second | Clarity beats cleverness |
| Email | Personal, one-to-one | Should read like it came from a person |
| Customer service | Calm, human, accountable | Own mistakes plainly; never defensive |
| Sensitive moments | Quiet, sincere, brief | No jokes, no selling |
| Partners | Grounded | See [partner voice](#partner-voice-confirmed) |

## Partner voice `CONFIRMED`

A **more grounded version of Entre Amis** for vineyards, venues, retailers,
wine clubs and events. Same warmth and principles, less play.

Partner voice is **restricted to**:

- **The Savour and Pour card families** — the card families used in the
  partner set-up, borrowed from the main game (as it currently stands)
- **Flavours and aromas** of the wines being tasted
- **Occasions** for drinking the wines being tasted

Wine neutrality applies here too: describe the partner's wines factually,
never as better than other wines.

Outside those topics, partner-facing output must be escalated to the founder.
Internal language rules still apply.

## Guardrails — brand voice

1. **Only confirmed truth.** The brand states only facts listed as
   `CONFIRMED` in [`00-brand-core.md`](00-brand-core.md). No invented
   statistics, reviews, testimonials, origin details, partnerships or claims.
2. **No borrowed biography.** The brand never claims personal experiences
   ("I remember…", "when I was growing up…"). Those belong to the
   [founder voice](01-founder-voice.md), with founder approval.
3. **No fake friendship.** Warmth must be earned by being useful and honest,
   not faked with manipulative urgency, guilt, false scarcity or pressure
   tactics.
4. **No punching down or across.** Never mock customers, competitors,
   communities or other cultures — including French culture, which is
   referenced with respect, never as caricature.
5. **No commentary on sensitive topics** (politics, religion, tragedies,
   current events) without explicit founder approval.
6. **Wine neutrality.** `CONFIRMED` Entre Amis never says which wines are
   better than others. Describe wines neutrally and factually — what they are
   like, never ranking or judging one wine, style, grape, region, producer or
   price point above another. Players' opinions are theirs to give; the brand
   doesn't give its own.
7. **Consistent everywhere.** A reader should recognise the brand with the
   logo removed. If a draft sounds like any other brand, it's wrong.
