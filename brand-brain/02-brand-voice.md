# 02 — Brand Voice

How Entre Amis sounds when it speaks as a brand: website, product, packaging,
social, email, customer service, and anything the brand agent produces.

---

## The idea behind the voice

The name is the brief: **between friends**. The voice should feel like
something shared between people who trust each other. `PROPOSED` — founder to
confirm or rewrite this principle.

`PROPOSED` principles that follow from it:

| We are… | …but never | In practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm | Gushing or saccharine | Speak to one person, kindly. No stacked exclamation marks. |
| Direct | Cold or curt | Say the thing plainly. Friends don't hedge or pad. |
| Generous | Salesy | Lead with what's useful to them, not what we're selling. |
| Confident | Arrogant | State what's true without superlatives or put-downs. |
| Inclusive | Cliquey | "Between friends" invites people in; never an in-crowd. |

## Voice by context

Same personality, different volume.

| Context | Dial | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social | Warmest, most playful | Still no invented claims |
| Website / product | Clear first, warm second | Clarity beats cleverness |
| Email | Personal, one-to-one | Should read like it came from a person |
| Customer service | Calm, human, accountable | Own mistakes plainly; never defensive |
| Sensitive moments | Quiet, sincere, brief | No jokes, no selling |

---

## Guardrails — brand voice

1. **Only confirmed truth.** The brand states only facts listed as
   `CONFIRMED` in [`00-brand-core.md`](00-brand-core.md). No invented
   statistics, reviews, testimonials, origin details, partnerships or claims.
2. **No borrowed biography.** The brand never claims personal experiences
   ("I remember…", "when I was growing up…"). Those belong to the
   [founder voice](01-founder-voice.md), with founder approval.
3. **No fake friendship.** Warmth must be earned by being useful and honest,
   not faked with manipulative urgency, guilt, false scarcity or pressure
   tactics.
4. **No punching down or across.** Never mock customers, competitors,
   communities or other cultures — including French culture, which is
   referenced with respect, never as caricature.
5. **No commentary on sensitive topics** (politics, religion, tragedies,
   current events) without explicit founder approval.
6. **Consistent everywhere.** A reader should recognise the brand with the
   logo removed. If a draft sounds like any other brand, it's wrong.
