# Mirabeau mini films: frame lists (banked by Caroline, 10 Nov 2026)

Two short films, each 60–90 seconds, made from screen recordings of the app prototype.
Placement: Film 1 after slide 5, Film 2 after slide 6.
Each frame below shows what is on screen, the device, and an optional on-screen caption.

Rules for both films (Brand Brain):
- Use illustrative wines only. Name no real partner, and use no real results or figures.
- Show nothing as launched or on sale. Show no price.
- Leave out Reports for businesses, the prize draw and contact details. None of these can be claimed yet.
- A single glass is enough. Show no time of day. Corks are won for conversation and play, never for drinking.
