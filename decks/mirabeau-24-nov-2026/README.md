## The meeting

- Caroline and Pete meet Stephen from Mirabeau. Tuesday 24 November 2026,
  10:30 proposed, Ham Yard Hotel lounge proposed.
- Material to Pete by Thursday 12 November 2026, then a preparation meeting.
- **Purpose (Caroline):** an informal, exploratory conversation in a public
  hotel lounge. Help Stephen understand that Entre Amis is a serious business
  solving a genuine problem, with an exciting consumer experience and
  potential commercial applications for wine producers. Share the concept,
  show what has been created, and ask for his honest advice and industry
  perspective. Introductions, partnerships or other opportunities may follow
  naturally.
- **Not:** an investment pitch, a formal business proposal, or a request to
  invest in or buy the business. (Brand Brain 00: investors are a deferred
  audience; no investor-specific material.)
- No partnership with Mirabeau exists or is implied. Mirabeau's name in Brand
  Brain 06 (themed editions) is illustrative only and must not appear in
  anything Stephen sees.

## Presentation requirements (Caroline, 10 November 2026)

- **The Full Story stays the single master** (Google Slides, Master v1.0,
  draft 6 November 2026, 51 slides). It is not split into other masters and
  is not changed unless Caroline authorises specific changes.
- The meeting uses a **meeting-specific selection of no more than eight
  slides**, built from existing Full Story slides wherever possible.
- Themes to cover (themes, not titles): 1 the problem and opportunity; 2 the
  vision (shared experiences, wine as the first expression); 3 the physical
  game; 4 the connected app (playing, tasting, discovery, remembering wines);
  5 the vineyard opportunity; 6 the broader business; 7 progress (built, in
  private testing, still to validate); 8 the conversation (where Stephen sees
  opportunities, challenges, areas to explore).
- For each slide: original number and title, purpose, unchanged or the
  specific amendment, claims needing qualification.
- No detailed financial projections, no confidential shareholder
  information, no invented commercial results. Visual, concise, credible.

## Video requirements (Caroline, 10 November 2026)

- Shown after the slides. About 90 seconds to two minutes.
- Shows the occasion (friends around wine), preparing and choosing wines, the
  physical game and cards, how the app supports it, real interaction and
  enjoyment, and how it concludes. Communicates how it feels, not only
  software features.
- Must work **without sound** (public space) and play **locally on a laptop**.
- At this stage: no generated images, no video production, no app changes.

## Caroline's decisions (10 November 2026, after review)

- Open with both the wider opportunity and the consumer problem: Full Story
  **40** Why now, then **3** Early consumer insight (problem and opportunity
  message kept; the 78% and 56% figures and the respondent quote removed from
  the meeting version), then **4** Conversation, not knowledge, as the
  response. The three work as one opening without repetition.
- Still no more than eight slides. The other five keep the strongest of: the
  game and the app; why Entre Amis matters to producers; the broader business
  and commercial potential (the commercial story is not dropped); where we are
  today; an invitation for Stephen's advice.
- Entre Amis stays only as a clearly labelled future ambition.
- Progress slide headings: **Created**, **Testing**, **Next to prove**. No
  tester count.
- Closing slide headed **"We'd value your view"** with three questions:
  1. Where do you see the greatest opportunity for something like this in the
     wine industry?
  2. What would you challenge or want us to prove?
  3. Who do you think we should be speaking to?
- Pete receives the eight slides as a PDF, and the video storyboard
  separately, by Thursday 12 November.
- A clearly labelled Mirabeau meeting-specific copy in the existing Google
  Drive folder is agreed in principle. The Full Story remains the single
  comprehensive master. Neither is to be edited or made until Caroline
  approves the final structure and wording.
- Video: keep the 100-second customer-experience approach, with real people
  and the actual app at its centre, shown before the closing slide. No
  filming schedule committed and nothing produced yet.
- Objective unchanged: a credible, interesting conversation showing a serious
  business solving a real problem; not an investment or acquisition pitch.
