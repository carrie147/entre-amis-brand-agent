# The Full Story: current master deck

**Current master:** `ENTRE AMIS - The Full Story - master - 29 October 2026 v8.pptx`
(41 slides). The text of every slide and its notes is in the matching `(text).md` file.

It is the internal working master, marked *Not for sharing*. Each slide's notes
say where the slide is used: Brand story, Partner Mode pack, Event Mode pack, or
internal master only. Shared versions are cut from this master, and the
internal-master label is removed from them.

## What changed from v7 (29 October 2026, founder)

- **Branding:** the designer's final master logo (from `brand-brain/assets/logo/`)
  replaces the September logo on every slide, including the cover.
- **Slide 3:** "Conversation, not knowledge." now has its comma, as in the Brand
  Brain, and the double space is gone.
- **Old slide 7 removed:** "The Entre Amis world" duplicated slide 6 (Two audiences,
  one connected brand). Slide 6 stays, and keeps stays as context. Later slides
  move up one, and the slide references in the text and notes follow.
- **Slide 9:** Just tasting is for anyone ("you can explore"), not only Hosts.
- **Slide 13 (Just tasting):** the front door wording matches the app since
  25 October (Play with friends · Scan or enter a code · Just tasting). The stand-in
  screens are replaced with Just tasting's own screens from the 29 October build.
- **Slide 20:** the last two promises now match slide 25: "No risk of identifying
  anyone" and "No data sharing across platforms".
- **Slide numbers** on every slide after the cover.
- The cover is dated 29 October 2026. Each change is logged in the slide's notes
  as a "29 OCT PASS".
