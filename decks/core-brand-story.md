## 2 · More connected. Not always closer.

**Why now, and why wine first**

Being in touch isn't the same as feeling close. The moments that stay with us
are usually conversations, discoveries and stories shared with other people.

Wine is one of life's everyday pleasures. Yet long wine lists and specialist
words can leave people unsure of their choice, and quiet about what they
really think.

| DIGITAL EVERYWHERE | TIME TOGETHER | WINE, FIRST |
| --- | --- | --- |
| We talk and discover through screens, but closeness happens in person. | A shared table gives people room to be present, curious and open. | A glass on the table is a natural place to start a conversation. |

**What we often hear**
You'll be better at choosing the wine than me.
People want to enjoy themselves, not prove themselves.

> following the unattributed observations rule (⚑ PROPOSED). I've left out
> "even in everyday pubs, people feel they need expertise" until you have

## 7 · The game

**A wine game for people who would rather have an opinion than know the answer.**

*Pour. Spin. Play. Talk.*

A beautifully made table game that turns any wine into conversation.

- **Savour**: the flavours, tastes and look of the wine.
- **Pour**: the occasions and places a wine might be enjoyed.
- **Mischief**: changes the course of the game.
- **Wild**: questions that simply get people talking.
- **Secret Missions**: private challenges, brought up by the spinner.

At the end, the table chooses its favourite wine together and gives it the crown.

| 2–8 PLAYERS | ANY WINE | ANY TIME OF DAY | NO APP NEEDED | 18+ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Easy to start, easy to join. | A single glass is enough to play. | Lunch or evening. | Complete on its own; the app adds more. | For adults only. |

Corks are won for conversation and play, never for how much anyone drinks.

*In development.*

## 8 · The app

**Works with the table, never instead of it.**

- Hosts and Guests play together.
- Runs the game, including a digital spinner.
- Plain-English hints when curiosity appears.
- Saves the occasion and the scores.
- **Wine 101**: wine facts and help, like a directory.
- **My wines**: your tastings, favourites, events and experiences, to look
  back on at any time.
- Optional Word Play and Wine Knowledge levels.
- Hosts can also use it for their own tasting.

18+ age gate on entry. *In development.*

> Notes: wine recognition is deliberately left out. It isn't claimable until

## 9 · For vineyards, retailers, wine clubs and wine events

**What we add isn't content. It's connection.**

Wine businesses already have stories, tasting notes and expertise. We help
capture what people say and really think about your wines, and help them
remember those wines afterwards.

| BETWEEN PEOPLE | WITH THE WINE | BEYOND THE MOMENT | WHAT PEOPLE REALLY THINK |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tasting becomes conversation and shared discovery. | People taste, talk and choose, so your wines become part of the occasion. | Wines and memories saved, so the relationship carries on. | In the words people naturally use. Anonymised, and only with consent. |

**Two ways in**
**Partner Mode**: for a vineyard, wine brand, retailer or wine club.
**Event Mode**: for organisers bringing several vineyards together.
**Meet the Maker** stories and videos work in both.

## 10 · Responsible by design

- **18+ only.** The game is clearly marked 18+, and the app has an age gate.
- **Never a drinking game.** Corks are won for conversation and play, never
  for how much anyone drinks.
- **A single glass is enough.** Any wine works.
- **No pressure to taste.** At events, tasting is always optional.
- **Safe to say what you think.** What people say is anonymised, needs their
  consent, and is never sold.

## 13 · Looking ahead: Entre Amis stays

**A future ambition.**

Not a tour. Not a retreat. More like staying with friends who know all the
good places. Entre Amis, lived for a few days rather than played for an
evening.

- Shared tables, never rigid itineraries.
- Everything is optional.
- Wine explained when you're curious, not taught at you.
- Local places chosen because we love them, not because they're on a checklist.
- Time to disappear for an afternoon if you want to.
- A relaxed Provençal home where you can wander, read and swim.

The setting changes. The spirit never does.
